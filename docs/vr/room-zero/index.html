ROOM ZERO

Stand in the room. Point a controller at the clay block and hold the trigger (or squeeze) to grab it — let go and it settles flat, snapped to the exact millimetre. The amber square is Big Forge's plate, 220 × 220 mm; the blue ghost on it stands 165.1 mm, the figure standard. Press EXPORT STL in the browser view to save the block as a printable file, and SNAPSHOT to save a picture of the room. All confirmations appear as text here — this room has no voice. On a computer: drag the block with the mouse.

Block: 100 × 60 × 60 mm
